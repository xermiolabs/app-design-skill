# Product presets

Pick the closest product type. Copy its defaults into the plan, then adjust for the brief. If the product mixes two types, take the dials of the one that matches the main job.

How to use the "study" list: open those apps (or their screens on Mobbin) and note specific decisions: how dense the home is, where the primary action sits, how color is used. Take decisions, not layouts. Never ship a recognizable clone.

---

## 1. Productivity and utilities

To-do, notes, calendar, scanners, file tools, timers, converters.

- **Dials:** density 3 to 4, color 1 to 2, motion 2.
- **Home answers:** what is next, today.
- **Color:** neutral chrome, one accent for actions and the current item. State colors only for meaning (overdue, done).
- **Type:** system font; strong hierarchy between item titles and metadata.
- **Must-haves:** quick add in thumb reach, search, swipe actions on rows, undo, widgets for the main glance.
- **Study:** Things 3 (restraint, quick entry), Todoist (dense lists that stay readable), Apple Notes (native everything).
- **Avoid:** stats dashboards on the home screen, onboarding longer than the first task, decorative illustrations.

## 2. Content and media

Music, podcasts, video, books, reading, photo browsing.

- **Dials:** density 4, color 4 (from content), motion 3.
- **Home answers:** continue where I left off, then what is new for me.
- **Color:** the chrome stays near black or near white; artwork, covers and photos carry the color and are shown large. Do not mute or tint the content to match a brand palette.
- **Type:** bold, compact section titles; metadata small and quiet.
- **Must-haves:** "continue" row at the top, large artwork, a persistent mini player that does not cover content, fast filter chips, downloads/offline state.
- **Study:** Apple Music (artwork scale, mini player), Pocket Casts (dense but calm lists), Letterboxd (content-led color).
- **Avoid:** cloning Spotify's layout, one giant hero card that pushes everything below the fold, gray boxes where art should be.

## 3. Finance, banking and budgeting

- **Dials:** density 4, color 2, motion 2.
- **Home answers:** how much do I have or can I spend, and what changed.
- **Color:** calm neutrals; green and red only for direction of money, always with a sign or arrow too.
- **Type:** tabular numbers everywhere; the key number is the biggest thing on screen; cents smaller.
- **Must-haves:** the key number, recent transactions with merchant names and categories, quick add or pay in thumb reach, clear pending vs settled, privacy toggle to hide balances.
- **Study:** Monzo (friendly clarity), Copilot Money (dense, calm data), Cash App (one bold action).
- **Avoid:** gradient credit cards, neon crypto styling, charts without a question they answer, any fake trust badges.

## 4. Health, fitness and habits

- **Dials:** density 3, color 3, motion 3 to 4 for completion moments.
- **Home answers:** how am I doing today against my goal.
- **Color:** one energetic accent for progress; a small set of category colors if activities differ.
- **Type:** big progress numbers, short labels.
- **Must-haves:** today's progress vs goal, one-tap logging, streaks or history, gentle reminders, widgets and watch glance where relevant.
- **Study:** Apple Fitness (rings as the single signature), Strava (activity detail), Streaks (one-tap logging).
- **Avoid:** guilt copy, cluttered stat grids, celebrations on every tap.

## 5. Wellness, meditation and sleep

- **Dials:** density 1 to 2, color 2 to 3 (soft), motion 3 but slow.
- **Home answers:** what should I do right now (one recommended session).
- **Color:** soft, low-contrast backgrounds with text still at 4.5:1; dark mode first for sleep content.
- **Type:** generous size and line height; a softer typeface is fine.
- **Must-haves:** one clear recommendation, duration on every item, offline audio, a quiet player.
- **Study:** Calm and Headspace (single recommendation), Oak (minimal timer).
- **Avoid:** dense lists, bright white screens at night, aggressive upsells mid-session.

## 6. Social, messaging and community

- **Dials:** density 4, color 2 (content brings color), motion 3.
- **Home answers:** what is new from people I care about, or my conversations.
- **Color:** neutral chrome; avatars and media bring color; one accent for the main action and unread state.
- **Type:** names in medium weight, content in body, timestamps quiet.
- **Must-haves:** composer reachable from everywhere, unread states, pull to refresh, clear reply and reaction targets, safety controls (block, report, mute).
- **Study:** Threads (sparse chrome), Telegram (fast, dense chats), iMessage (native conversation patterns).
- **Avoid:** cloning Instagram, heavy borders around every post, engagement-bait badges.

## 7. Commerce, shopping and food delivery

- **Dials:** density 4, color 3, motion 2.
- **Home answers:** find what I want fast, or reorder what I had.
- **Color:** product photos bring color; one accent for add to cart and checkout.
- **Type:** prices prominent and tabular; old prices struck and muted.
- **Must-haves:** search at the top, categories, reorder or recently viewed, sticky cart or checkout bar, clear delivery time and fees before checkout.
- **Study:** Airbnb (photography-led cards, clear price), DoorDash (fast reorder), the Apple Store app (calm product pages).
- **Avoid:** hidden fees revealed late, carousels of banners, countdown timers that pressure.

## 8. Travel, maps and mobility

- **Dials:** density 3, color 2 to 3, motion 3.
- **Home answers:** where am I going and when.
- **Color:** the map is the background; UI sits in a bottom sheet with neutral surfaces; one accent for the route or trip.
- **Must-haves:** map-first layout with a draggable bottom sheet, live status, times in large type, offline essentials.
- **Study:** Apple Maps (sheet over map), Citymapper (clear options), Flighty (status-first).
- **Avoid:** full-screen lists that hide the map, tiny times, modal stacks.

## 9. Education, learning and kids

- **Dials:** density 2 to 3, color 4 to 5, motion 4.
- **Home answers:** continue my lesson.
- **Color:** a bright, friendly palette is right here, still with accessible contrast.
- **Type:** rounded or friendly sans; larger sizes; very short instructions.
- **Must-haves:** one big "continue" action, visible progress, immediate feedback on answers, parent controls for kids' apps, tap targets larger than the minimum for young children.
- **Study:** Duolingo (lesson before sign-up, feedback), Brilliant (interactive explanation), Khan Academy Kids (child-sized targets).
- **Avoid:** text-heavy screens, small targets, ads or dark patterns in kids' apps.

## 10. AI assistants and chat apps

- **Dials:** density 2 to 3, color 2, motion 2 to 3.
- **Home answers:** ask something now.
- **Color:** neutral, quiet; one accent for send and active states.
- **Must-haves:** composer pinned to the bottom above the keyboard, streaming responses with a stop button, copy and share on messages, history reachable from the top, attachments and voice in the composer.
- **Study:** ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity apps (composer-first layouts).
- **Avoid:** a splash of suggestion cards that pushes the composer down, typing animations slower than the text arrives.

## 11. Business tools, admin and field apps

Team tools, CRMs, POS, inventory, internal apps.

- **Dials:** density 5, color 1 to 2, motion 1.
- **Home answers:** what needs my attention.
- **Color:** almost monochrome; color only for status.
- **Must-haves:** lists with filters and saved views, fast search, offline tolerance, large targets for use while standing or moving, confirmation for destructive actions.
- **Study:** Linear mobile (dense, quiet), Slack (navigation between many spaces), Shopify POS (big touch targets).
- **Avoid:** marketing-style screens, decorative charts, long onboarding.
