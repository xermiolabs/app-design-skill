# Copy

Words are part of the interface. Most screens that feel off have a design problem and a copy problem at the same time.

## Interface copy

- Write like a helpful person, not a system. "We couldn't save your changes" instead of "Save operation failed".
- Sentence case everywhere: buttons, headings, menus, labels.
- Buttons say what happens: "Create invoice", "Send 3 messages", "Start free trial". Never "Submit", "OK", "Click here".
- Keep labels short and concrete. "Email" not "Your email address here".
- Errors: what happened, then what to do. "Card declined. Try another card or contact your bank."
- Empty states: what this place is, why it is empty, what to do next (see `components.md`).
- Numbers and times for humans: "3 min ago", "Oct 30", "12.4K views".
- Cut words. If a sentence still works without a word, remove the word.
- No exclamation marks in system messages. No "Oops!". No jokes in error messages.
- No fake personalization ("Welcome back, John!") unless the app really knows the name and it helps.

## Landing page copy

Above the fold must answer three questions in a few seconds:

1. What is this?
2. Who is it for?
3. What do I do next?

Rules:

- Headline: the outcome, in 12 words or fewer. "Know where every dollar goes" beats "The next-generation AI-powered finance platform".
- Subhead: one sentence that says how, or for whom.
- One conversion goal per page. The same primary call to action repeated two or three times down the page.
- Benefits over features. "Get paid in 2 days" over "Automated invoicing engine".
- Specific over clever. Numbers, names and concrete results over wordplay.
- Use the customer's words. Read reviews, support emails and Reddit threads; reuse the phrases people actually say.
- Proof only if it is real: real numbers, real quotes with names. Never invent testimonials, logos, user counts or ratings.
- Calls to action that state the next step: "Start free trial", "Book a demo", "Get access", "Download for Mac".

## Words that make copy sound generated

Avoid: revolutionize, seamless, unlock, elevate, empower, supercharge, game-changing, cutting-edge, next-generation, leverage, robust, effortless, "in today's fast-paced world", "say goodbye to", "look no further", "take your X to the next level".

If a sentence could appear on any product's website, rewrite it until it could only appear on this one.
