# Inspiration and references

When you are unsure how something should look, do not invent it. Find a real product that solves the same problem well, study why it works, and adapt the principle (not the pixels).

## Real product screens and flows

- Mobbin (mobbin.com): the largest library of real iOS, Android and web app screens and flows. Search by pattern: onboarding, paywall, settings, empty state.
- SaaS Landing Page (saaslandingpage.com) and Saaspo (saaspo.com): real SaaS marketing sites by section.
- Land-book (land-book.com): landing pages across industries.

## Principles

- Laws of UX (lawsofux.com): Fitts, Hick, Jakob, proximity and the rest, with examples.
- Nielsen Norman Group's 10 usability heuristics (nngroup.com).
- Refactoring UI by Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger: practical visual design for developers.
- Apple Human Interface Guidelines (developer.apple.com/design) and Material Design 3 (m3.material.io) for native apps.
- Emil Kowalski (emilkowal.ski): animation and interaction craft for the web.

## Components

- The Component Gallery (component.gallery): how dozens of design systems name and build the same component.
- shadcn/ui (ui.shadcn.com): accessible, copy-in React components.

## Type

- Google Fonts and Fontshare for free fonts; Typewolf (typewolf.com) for pairings seen on real sites.

## Products worth studying for polish

Linear (restraint, density, keyboard-first), Stripe (documentation and dashboards), Vercel (typography and dark UI), Apple (hierarchy and product pages), Things 3 (native iOS and macOS craft), Arc and Raycast (macOS interaction detail).

Study them for decisions: how much space, how few colors, how quiet the secondary text is. Do not copy their layouts.
